# Fill and AutoFill

The **fill handle** is the small square at the bottom-right corner of the active
cell. Dragging it copies data or continues a pattern.

## Copying with the fill handle

1. Type a value in a cell.
2. Hover the bottom-right corner until the cursor becomes a thin `+`.
3. Drag down or across.

## Continuing a pattern

Excel recognises sequences and continues them automatically.

| You enter | Dragging gives |
| --- | --- |
| `Monday` | Tuesday, Wednesday, ... |
| `Jan` | Feb, Mar, Apr, ... |
| `1, 2` | 3, 4, 5, ... |
| `2, 4` | 6, 8, 10, ... |
| `Q1` | Q2, Q3, Q4, ... |

## Double-click to fill

If the column next to your data is continuous, **double-click** the fill handle
to fill down to match its length. This is much faster than dragging.

## Fill options

After dragging, a small **AutoFill Options** button appears. Use it to choose:

- Copy Cells
- Fill Series
- Fill Formatting Only
- Fill Without Formatting

## Flash Fill

**Flash Fill** (`Ctrl + E`) detects a pattern from examples. If `A1` is
`John Smith` and you type `John` in `B1`, Flash Fill can extract the first name
for the whole column.

## Key takeaways

- The fill handle copies and continues patterns.
- Double-click the handle to fill down automatically.
- `Ctrl + E` uses Flash Fill to split or combine text by example.

> **Practice:** Type `1` in `A1` and `2` in `A2`, select both, then drag the fill
> handle to `A10`. Excel continues the sequence.
