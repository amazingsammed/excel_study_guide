1. Data stored on your device

The App is designed to work offline. We do not operate a user account system or a server that receives your study data. The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • topics you bookmark;
  • your best quiz scores;
  • your theme and text-size preferences;
  • whether you have completed the introduction.

This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.

The App itself does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files. The app declares internet access, which is required to request ads. Information handled by the App and its advertising provider is used to:

We do not use your bookmarks, quiz scores, or preferences for advertising, and we do not sell them.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the study data stored locally on your device. Third-party advertising partners, including Google, may collect and share information as described in their own privacy policies for the advertising purposes described above.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored study data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. We do not keep server-side copies of that data. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies and your Google account and advertising settings.
